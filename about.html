About me:

About me:

Hello! I am White, a freelance artist with anime styled art! I am from Hong Kong. I mostly self-taught myself how to draw, I've been drawing for my whole life but I only started taking art seriously at around 2021.


What do I like:

I like to watch anime, draw and do my passion projects in my freetime! Because I am often free, I have a lot of time to do many things. I love to write, world build and create characters. It is a truly fasinating thing to see my characters come to life.


My dreams:

My dream is to become a full-time freelance artist, I know people like looking at my art. I want to further improve my skills in writing and drawing, so I can create even more immersive writing and improve my world building skills. I also want to make games if I have the skills to do so.
